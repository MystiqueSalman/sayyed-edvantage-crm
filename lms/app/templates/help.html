Help & FAQs

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I enroll in a course?

Browse the course catalog, open a course and choose Enroll. You can also send an enquiry and our counsellor will call you back.

Where is my certificate?

Complete all lessons and pass every module quiz — your certificate appears under My Learning → Certificates, and can be downloaded as a PDF.

How do I join a live class?

Open your dashboard → Live Classes (or the calendar). The Join button becomes active 15 minutes before the session starts.

I forgot my password. What now?

Use Forgot password on the login page, or sign in with an email code.
